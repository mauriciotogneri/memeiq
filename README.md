# Meme IQ

A browser game that tests how well you know internet memes. Each stage plays a meme's audio clip and you pick the matching meme from a set of thumbnails. Answer correctly and quickly to earn a higher IQ score.

Play it at [meme-iq.web.app](https://meme-iq.web.app).

## How to play

1. Choose how many memes to play (10, 30, or the whole catalogue) and a difficulty.
2. Each stage plays a meme's audio. Pick the thumbnail that matches it.
3. When the match ends, you get your IQ score. Open **REVIEW** to see each meme you played, marked correct or missed, and replay its clip.

| # | Difficulty | Cards per stage |
|---|------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Easy       | 3               |
| 2 | Medium     | 4               |
| 3 | Hard       | 6               |

### Scoring

The highest possible score is 150 IQ, split evenly across the match's memes. A correct pick earns its share minus one point for every 3 seconds you took to answer, and never less than zero. A wrong pick earns nothing.
